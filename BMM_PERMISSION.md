Granted by TraditionalDimension for the official Showstreak releases. All other restrictions in [LICENSE](LICENSE) remain in effect.

### Limited permission for Balatro Mod Manager

As an exception to the redistribution restriction above, TraditionalDimension permits Showstreak to be listed in balatro-mod-index and permits Balatro Mod Manager to download, distribute and install unmodified official Showstreak release archives for use in Balatro. This permission covers the author's Showstreak code, documentation and author-owned resources only, preserves the accompanying credits and terms, and does not permit modified releases, unrelated mirrors, resale or inclusion in modpacks. It grants no rights to third-party works.

This exception also applies to the official Showstreak 1.0.0 player archive released by TraditionalDimension, even where its bundled LICENSE predates this exception.
